## 1. Strategic creative brief
- **Concept:** MODULA, fictional desk-organizer prop; no verified SKU, materials, dimensions, load capacity, price, stock or sale.
- **Objective:** demonstrate creative UGC storytelling, product visibility and variant readiness for an e-commerce portfolio; NOT a published sales ad or proof of performance.
- **Audience hypothesis (not verified research):** Spanish-speaking home-office workers/students who visually appreciate a tidy desk.
- **Problem:** visually cluttered desk; momentary search friction, dramatized *as a scenario*, not quantified as a common outcome.
- **Desire:** a calmer-looking, more intentional desk surface.
- **Angle:** "¿Dónde quedó?" — visible search → arrangement → quick visual reveal.
- **Central proposition:** watch the fictional desk reset. **No attribution** of measured time saved, productivity, ergonomics, or superior performance.
- **Visual hook:** hand searches among pens/notebook/cable in authentic overhead frame; interruption at 0.0 seconds.
- **Verbal hook A:** "¿Dónde quedó mi pluma? Si tu mesa se ve así, mira."
- **Tension:** viewer expects to see the missing item while the camera shows the clutter.
- **Demonstration:** a hand *places* familiar items into a stable conceptual organizer prop; show only visibly depicted object placement, no unverified compartments or material properties. Mechanical fidelity needs approved reference frames.
- **Observable payoff:** original pluma is visibly found in the staged scene. Not a causal claim of efficiency.
- **CTA:** "Mira el concepto MODULA." Never "cómpralo", discount, product listing or live shopping button for a fictional SKU.
- **Style:** tactile premium everyday UGC, not glossy 3D CGI; low-key observational opening, daylight transformation, subtle imperfection.
- **Presenter:** adult fictional consent-cleared synthetic presenter, Mexican-neutral accent, natural cadence; hands-only B-roll in scenes 2–5. Avoid claiming personal usage.
- **Pacing:** 0–3s immediate friction, 3–10s reveal, 10–23s placement/reveal, 23–30s close; hard cuts, no morph transitions.
- **Visual rules:** 9:16; design for 1080×1920 final, but provider source may be 720p; no false native-1080p claim. Text/brand/disclosure should be added in post, not asked of video model.
- **Audio:** quiet original/licensed warm percussive bed, 90–110 BPM *creative suggestion only*; prop and paper foley. Duck under dialogue. License not acquired.
- **Captions:** Spanish with readable 2-line maximum, high-contrast inside safe area; hard-coded from transcript with timestamp review.
- **Disclosures:** clearly display "Demo conceptual · Producto ficticio" in readable end-card and portfolio caption; add AI-generated content disclosure per platform policy and config when distribution is planned.
- **Commercial boundaries:** do not claim any third-party mark, testimonials, real product performance, sales, customer outcomes or product-specific technical features.
