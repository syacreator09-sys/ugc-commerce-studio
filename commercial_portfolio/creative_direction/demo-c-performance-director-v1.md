# CANO UGC — LUMO | Performance Creative Direction V1
**Campaign:** `demo-c-performance` · **Source:** PR #11 `commercial_portfolio/campaigns.json` · **Status:** PREPRODUCTION ONLY. **Product:** fictional LUMO reading lamp. No confirmed lux, lumens, power, battery, energy savings, LED type, attachment, price, stock or technical certification.

## 1. Creative brief
- **Objective:** demonstrate modular direct-response creative experimentation for a fictional reading lamp, not actual sales or ad performance.
- **Audience hypothesis:** LATAM readers/students and apartment dwellers who like intentionally designed reading corners; unverified.
- **Problem:** a reading corner looks visually flat in the staged shot; **do not** claim it harms eyesight or that a lamp medically helps.
- **Desire:** mood and a cozy visual composition.
- **Angle:** "Mismo rincón, dos encuadres de luz." Visual pattern interruption and a clearly disclosed staged lighting scene.
- **Big idea:** different creative treatments of the same corner, with a consistent LUMO **concept prop**. Do not suggest a regulated or measured output.
- **Visual hook:** jump from dim-looking staged corner to nicely staged ambient scene with lamp silhouette; **post-stage disclosure**, never fake evidence of real lamp brightness.
- **Verbal hook primary:** "¿Qué le falta a este rincón? Mira dónde pusimos la luz."
- **Narrative tension:** identify visual imbalance, then reveal deliberate lamp positioning rather than claim technical performance.
- **Demonstration:** an adult hand manually positions an approved fictional lamp next to the same navy book and cream chair; show switched-light visual **only as composited scene** (do not attribute lumen output to fictitious SKU).
- **Observable benefit:** aesthetic effect of art-directed composition only; not proof of light level or energy consumption.
- **CTA:** "Explora el concepto LUMO."
- **Presenter:** primarily hands-only, one short on-camera adult LATAM presenter *optional*. Prefer speech with narrator if workflow supports voice over B-roll. No personal experience or recommendation claim.
- **Look:** realistic premium apartment UGC: cream textured wall, warm-neutral wooden side table, beige upholstered chair, navy book, lamp prop LUMO-PROP-01 only from approved geometry still, small pale curtain.
- **Camera:** 9:16, stable 35mm equivalent wide plus product-close 50mm; fixed position for visual compare, no artificial zoom flares.
- **Light:** general soft daylight and practicals art-directed; if simulated lamp glow, label as creative illustration, not technical proof.
- **Audio:** original/licensed minimal rhythm with tactile click and page turn; mix for sound-off comprehension, no copyrighted library assumed.
- **Overlays:** high contrast 1–2 short lines, safe margins; no generated typography.
- **Compliance:** fictional product label throughout portfolio, AI disclosure per final channel; no claims of energy saving, certification, comfort/eye health or sale availability.
