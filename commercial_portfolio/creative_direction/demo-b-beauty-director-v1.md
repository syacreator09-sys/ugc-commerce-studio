**Campaign:** `demo-b-beauty` · **Source:** PR #11 `commercial_portfolio/campaigns.json` · **Status:** PREPRODUCTION ONLY · **Product:** fictional NUBA cosmetic mist.
**Truth boundary:** brand, formulation, ingredients, safety, effects, fragrance, hydration, cruelty-free status, skin compatibility, price and availability are UNKNOWN. Never depict a verified application to skin, efficacy or testimonial. Do not use real packaging/logos.

## 1. Creative brief
- **Goal:** premium beauty/lifestyle **portfolio** storytelling and art direction, not sale or medical/cosmetic effectiveness.
- **Audience hypothesis:** LATAM women and adults drawn to low-key ritual aesthetics; not validated user research.
- **Problem/tension:** noisy, overstylized beauty advertising; attention split across daily objects.
- **Desire:** a visually calm, intentional vanity ritual.
- **Angle:** "No es otro tutorial de skincare" — an intimate personal *staging* moment, not a product recommendation.
- **Big idea:** the camera notices small tactile beauty ritual details; NUBA is a **fictional prop** in a visual editorial.
- **Primary visual hook:** condensation on mirror, drop of water on vanity (not on face), fingertip moves clutter aside revealing fictional bottle.
- **Primary verbal hook:** "¿Y si tu rutina empezara con un segundo de calma?"
- **Narrative tension:** close-ups hide the product silhouette for first 3 seconds, then meaningful prop reveal.
- **Demonstration:** close-up of bottle hand placement, rotation and coordinated vanity setup. **No skin contact, mist spraying, ingredient, absorption or effect depiction** until product evidentiary clearance.
- **Observable value:** the visual composition transforms from busy to intentional; it is *art direction*, not a product health/beauty benefit.
- **CTA:** "Conoce el concepto visual NUBA".
- **Presenter:** fictional adult woman, LATAM Spanish natural accent, lifestyle UGC with one frontal spoken beat + close B-roll hands. No personal use, no fake reviews.
- **Art:** vanity marble cream/limestone, warm off-white wall, beige cotton towel, unlabeled fictional frosted champagne bottle NUBA-PROP-01 with geometry only from approved reference; low-saturation champagne, cream, taupe; natural window light 45° screen right.
- **Camera:** 9:16, static 35/50mm-equivalent plus intentional macro inserts; realistic phone-cam with fine imperfection, no excessive beauty retouch.
- **Audio:** low sparse original/licensed ambient piano/pads, glass/cloth foley, soft spoken voice; music must be license-cleared.
- **Text:** elegant legible captions in edit, no generated packaging typography or illegible captions.
- **Disclosure:** "Demo conceptual · Marca ficticia" in end card and portfolio description. AI content disclosure per channel requirements and repo policies.
- **Claims:** never say moisturizes, repairs, safe for sensitive skin, clinically proven, dermatological, vegan, organic, reduces wrinkles, feels refreshing or smells floral.
